# Updating L.I.F.E. Ministry

Open `/admin` and sign in with your ministry password.

## Wednesday and Sunday

On **This Week**, choose **View or update** under the gathering you want.

- Sunday normally runs **11:30 AM–1:00 PM Eastern**.
- Wednesday normally runs **7:30–9:30 PM Eastern**.
- The website switches to the next gathering when the current one ends. Daylight saving time is handled automatically.
- A gathering continues each week even if you have not entered a sermon title. The website uses the gathering name until you add that date’s details.

Check the date, enter the message title and Scripture, and check the meeting link and preview before publishing. Wednesday and Sunday can have different links. Cancel a date when you are not gathering that week.

## A recording after the message

In the gathering editor, go to **Past recordings**, select the past gathering, paste its recording link, and save it. This updates the recording without replacing the next gathering.

## Homepage TikToks

Choose **Pastor’s TikToks** on the admin home page. Open a video on TikTok in your browser and copy its full address, including `/@name/video/` and the video number. Paste it into **TikTok video link**, add an optional title, choose **Add to list**, then **Save videos**.

You can edit titles and remove videos here. Removing a video from this list does not delete it from TikTok. These are chosen videos; new TikTok posts do not automatically appear on the website.

If another tab saved changes first, your changes stay on screen and the site asks you to reload the saved list. Keep any text you want to reuse before choosing to reload.

## Other tools

The admin home also links to the prayer and welcome inbox, events, giving, Daily Scripture, flyers, and messaging. These tools retain their own save and confirmation steps.
